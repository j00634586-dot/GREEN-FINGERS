🌱 Green Fingers

Green Fingers is an AI-powered agriculture assistant designed to help farmers identify crop and leaf problems quickly. Users can upload a crop leaf image to get a diagnosis, confidence score, visual explanation, and treatment guidance.

✨ Key Features
🌿 Crop leaf image upload & analysis
🤖 AI-based crop health diagnosis
📊 Confidence score and visual evidence
💊 Treatment & prevention guidance
🌦️ Weather risk assessment
📚 Agricultural knowledge base
❓ Interactive Farmer FAQs
📝 Scan history for previous analyses
🎯 Goal

Our goal is to make crop disease detection faster, simpler, and more accessible, helping farmers take informed action and protect their crops.

Tech Stack: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Flask
