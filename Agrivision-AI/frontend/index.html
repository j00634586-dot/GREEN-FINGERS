Farmer FAQs

Common questions about crop health and diagnosis.

Please mention the crop you are growing, such as Tomato, Potato, Rice, Wheat or Cotton. If you know the variety, mention it because different varieties can show different symptoms.

Describe symptoms such as yellow leaves, brown spots, black spots, curling, wilting, holes, white powder or discoloration. You can also upload a clear leaf image.

Tell us approximately when the symptoms first appeared, for example today, 3 days ago, one week ago or more than two weeks ago.

Mention whether only a few plants, one section, half of the field or most of the field is affected. This helps estimate the severity of the problem.

Mention recent rain, humidity, heat, cold, strong winds or unusual weather changes. High humidity and prolonged leaf wetness can increase the risk of several crop diseases.

Mention the product or treatment used, the quantity if known and when it was applied. This helps avoid unnecessary repeated treatments.

Green Fingers provides AI-assisted analysis of an uploaded crop-leaf image along with prediction, confidence, visual evidence and treatment guidance.

Review the AI evidence, treatment advisory and weather risk. For important treatment decisions, verify the result with a local agriculture expert.

Confidence represents how strongly the system matches visible image features with a detected category. A confidence score does not guarantee that the diagnosis is correct.

Upload a clear, well-lit image of the affected leaf. Avoid blurry images, extreme shadows and images where the leaf is too small.
